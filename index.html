    Formula

    Formula, mass, stereo (R/S, E/Z) and SMILES are calculated in the page and are reliable. Names are looked up in PubChem (NIH). For structures PubChem doesn’t have, the app names them with its own IUPAC 2013 rules (chains, rings, benzene and the common functional groups). Every name is checked with OPSIN, which reads it back into a structure. When the app can’t name something, it says so rather than guessing.
